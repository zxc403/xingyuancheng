# Asset Attribution

This file lists the third-party 3D assets bundled in this repository and their licenses.
All assets below are **free for commercial and personal use** under the stated terms.

## Kenney Game Assets

- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets
- **License:** CC0 (Public Domain)
- **Used packs:**
  - `car-kit` — cars, trucks, emergency vehicles, wheels, debris, cones
  - `city-kit-commercial` — commercial buildings, skyscrapers, awnings, parasols
  - `city-kit-suburban` — suburban houses, fences, paths, planters, trees
  - `city-kit-roads` — modular roads, bridges, intersections, street lights, signs
- **Note:** Files are copied unmodified from the original Kenney packs and organized into sub-folders.

## flo-bit Low Poly Asset Packs

- **Source:** https://github.com/flo-bit/low-poly-asset-packs
- **License:** CC0 (Public Domain)
- **Used pack:**
  - `nature-pack/glb` — palm trees, common trees, pine trees, bushes, rocks, grass, flowers, clouds
- **Note:** Only the `.glb` files from the `nature-pack` are included.
